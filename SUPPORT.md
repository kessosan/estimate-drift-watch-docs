# Estimate Drift Watch for Jira — Support

Estimate Drift Watch for Jira is a lightweight Forge app for Jira Cloud that helps teams identify estimate drift by comparing an issue's first known estimate with its current forecast.

## Getting help

For support requests, bug reports, or questions about the app, please open a GitHub issue:

https://github.com/kessosan/estimate-drift-watch-docs/issues

When reporting a problem, please include:

- your Jira Cloud site type;
- the affected issue key or project key, when safe to share;
- the expected result;
- the actual result;
- screenshots or error messages, if relevant;
- whether the issue is reproducible.

Please do not include passwords, API tokens, or other secrets in support requests.

## Support hours

Support is provided on French business days, typically between 09:00 and 18:00 CET/CEST.

## Target response times

- Critical issue preventing normal use of the app: initial response target within 1 business day.
- Functional bug or degraded behavior: initial response target within 2 business days.
- General question or feature request: initial response target within 3 business days.

These are response targets, not contractual service-level agreements.

## App status

Estimate Drift Watch runs on Atlassian Forge and does not depend on an external application backend. Service availability may therefore also depend on Atlassian Cloud and Forge availability.

## Security and privacy

The app does not use external analytics, advertising trackers, or an external application database for Jira issue data.
